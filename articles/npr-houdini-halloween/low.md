# 100 Years Later, Houdini's Death Still Haunts Detroit

Harry Houdini could get out of anything. Chains, locked boxes, locked rooms — he always escaped, in front of thousands of people. He was the most famous escape man in the world.

In 1926 he came to Detroit with a new show. Before the shows, a young man hit him hard in the stomach — several times, without warning. Houdini was hurt inside. He was in great pain, but he did not stop his shows. Then he fell. Doctors found his appendix had burst. Two operations could not save him. He died on Halloween — October 31, 1926.

His wife went to meetings where people tried to speak with the dead. She wanted to hear his ghost one more time. But no ghost came. "He always said the dead cannot come back," she said. "This proves he was right."

Some people in Detroit say his ghost is still there. This Halloween is 100 years since he died. Listen carefully — maybe the great escape man will escape one last time.
