# Ukraine and the U.S. Clash Over Starshield. What to Know

The leader of Ukraine and the U.S. government are in a serious fight. They are arguing about Starshield, an internet service from space that the Ukrainian army needs for its war.

## What Happened?

President Zelenskyy of Ukraine says his army suddenly lost its normal service last week. He believes the U.S. government gave the order, and that the space company SpaceX followed it. He called the results "very painful and unpleasant."

Both President Trump and Secretary of State Marco Rubio say this is not true. Trump said: "It wasn't me, and I don't think it was Elon." Rubio said the idea that the White House ordered a slowdown is simply false.

## What Is Starshield?

Starlink is a space internet network built by Elon Musk's company SpaceX. Starshield is its special version for the army. Since 2024, the U.S. has shared this system with Ukraine, and it has cost billions of dollars.

Why does it matter so much? Ukraine uses it to fight its almost five-year war against Russia. Zelenskyy explained: soldiers use it to bring down enemy planes, to hit far-away targets, and to send food and water to soldiers at the front line.

## Why Now?

The two countries have been moving apart for weeks. Trump wants the war to end, and he recently lifted some punishments on Russia. Zelenskyy called that a "weak decision." Trump then got angry and asked why Ukraine keeps hitting Russian oil plants.

Ukraine answered with action: its flying machines attacked oil targets inside Russia again this weekend.

A group of U.S. lawmakers wants a full look into who slowed the service down.
