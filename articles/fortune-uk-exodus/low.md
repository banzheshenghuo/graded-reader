# Not Just Rich People: a Record 69,300 Workers Have Left the U.K.

Last year, about 69,300 people told the UK tax office that they were leaving the country for good. That is one in three more than the year before — and a record number.

Rich people have been leaving Britain for a while. But money is not the only reason normal people move abroad, a new report shows.

## It Is Not Really About Money

The UK government has raised many taxes in recent years. So most people think that tax is why people leave.

But Natasha Stanley, who helps people change jobs for a living, told Fortune she cannot remember one single client who left the UK because of tax.

So why do people go? Her answer: for a better life. "Lots of people are moving for lifestyle purposes and more sunshine," she said.

Some move to live with a partner from another country. Others are worried about the UK's health service (the NHS). It is having a hard time, so they look for countries where public services work better.

## The Numbers Agree With Her

But for most people, the real problem is simple: the grey British weather. She says many of her clients have happily moved to Spain and Switzerland.

One big survey asks people who move abroad about their life. Spain just came first in that survey for the fifth year in a row. One in five people there said a better life was their main reason for moving. About 89% are happy with the weather, and 83% with the doctors and hospitals.

It is not about jobs: Spain is near the bottom of the list when it comes to work.

And rich people? For them too, money is not everything. One company that helps rich people move says safety, weather, and good hospitals are the usual reasons. Its last report said the UK health system is getting worse. It also pointed to growing safety worries in big cities like London.

The steel boss Lakshmi Mittal, a billionaire, has just left Britain after 30 years and moved to the Swiss mountains — where other very rich people now live too.
